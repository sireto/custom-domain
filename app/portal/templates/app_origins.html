Register an origin

The host the edge connects to. It must be reachable from this server and serve your product for any workspace. You get a verification token to serve from it next.

Leave the port empty for the scheme's default (443 or 80). Choose "Origin's own name" when the origin is behind a CDN (such as Cloudflare's proxy) or a reverse proxy that routes by Host: the customer's hostname then travels in X-Forwarded-Host.
