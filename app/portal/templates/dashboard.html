Get started

Three steps to serve your first customer hostname.

  2. 2

    Connect its backend

    Register the origin the edge forwards requests to, and prove you control it.

  3. 3

    Register customer hostnames

    Your product does this through the API or SDK; each customer then adds two DNS records.
